Introduction to Design of Experiment (DOE)

Learn two-level full factorial DOE by manipulating the design space. Start with a 2² design, then build a 2³ design, calculate main and interaction effects, edit the data, isolate cube planes, remove model terms, and inspect every treatment combination.
