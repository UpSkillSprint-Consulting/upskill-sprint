Reliability Formula Encyclopedia

Search the numbered equations in The ASQ Certified Reliability Engineer Handbook by Hulting and McShane-Vaughn. They are grouped into the five scored CRE sections. Each card is an original study note for that equation, not a copy of the handbook text. Risk management is included because it is a scored section, even though those chapters do not number an equation.

I. Reliability Fundamentals

Numbered measures of life, failure rate, repair, and availability from Chapters 1 and 2.

II. Risk Management

Chapters 3, 4, and 5 describe hazard analysis, FMEA, fault trees, and control selection. This edition does not assign equation numbers in those chapters. When a risk study needs a probability, a rate, or an availability, use the numbered formula in the matching section.

III. Probability and Statistics for Reliability

Numbered formulas for data, probability, regression, and life functions from Chapters 6 and 7.

IV. Reliability Planning, Testing, and Modeling

Numbered formulas for accelerated tests and reliability models from Chapters 9 and 10.

V. Lifecycle Reliability

Numbered formulas for demonstration, safety margin, availability, and spare parts from Chapters 11, 12, and 13.
