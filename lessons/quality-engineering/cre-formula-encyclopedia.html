Reliability Formula Encyclopedia

Scope summary

Search the formulas in The ASQ Certified Reliability Engineer Handbook by Hulting and McShane-Vaughn, including the numbered equations and the governing displays that have no equation number. They are grouped into the five scored CRE sections. Each card is an original study note, not a copy of the handbook text.

I. FundamentalsLife, failure rate, repair, and availability.
II. Risk managementNo displayed equation in Chapters 3 to 5.
III. ProbabilityData, probability, regression, and life functions.
IV. Planning and modelingAccelerated tests and reliability models.
V. LifecycleDemonstration, availability, and spare parts.

Every numbered equation is one card, and so is each governing formula the handbook displays without a number. A worked numerical substitution is not a separate card. Risk management stays visible because it is a scored section, and Chapters 3 to 5 still have no equation.
