Reliability Formula Encyclopedia

Scope summary

Search the numbered equations in The ASQ Certified Reliability Engineer Handbook by Hulting and McShane-Vaughn. They are grouped into the five scored CRE sections. Each card is an original study note, not a copy of the handbook text.

I. FundamentalsLife, failure rate, repair, and availability.
II. Risk managementNo numbered equation in Chapters 3 to 5.
III. ProbabilityData, probability, regression, and life functions.
IV. Planning and modelingAccelerated tests and reliability models.
V. LifecycleDemonstration, availability, and spare parts.

Every numbered equation in that handbook is one card. A worked example that the book does not number is not a separate card. Risk management stays visible because it is a scored section.
