Engineering Data Science Analysis Protocol

Use a disciplined, gated workflow to convert engineering data into statistically defensible, physically credible, reproducible, and decision-ready recommendations.

What you will learn

  • Frame an engineering decision before selecting a method.
  • Audit grain, lineage, measurement quality, confounding, and leakage.
  • Select and validate models that match process structure.
  • Translate output into operational risk and action.
  • Use AI prompts without surrendering technical judgment.

How to use this lesson

Work through the phases in order. Treat each gate as a stop/go review. Use the phase prompts as reusable AI instructions, then verify every output against process knowledge and source evidence.

Workflow map

From business question to sustained decision

Intake→Define→Acquire→Quality gate→Explore→Engineer→Model→Validate→Interpret→Decide→Document
