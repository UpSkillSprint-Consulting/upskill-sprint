Interactive I-MR signal lab

Paste one chronological continuous observation per line or separate values with commas. The lab calculates an educational I-MR chart, Test 1 signals, and a nine-point same-side run. It is not a replacement for Minitab’s complete methods and options.

Minimum: 10 valid numeric observations.

Assumptions: values are time ordered, consecutive observations are not strongly autocorrelated, one stable measurement system is used, and the data represent one process unless stages are intentionally defined.

Interpretation guide: when you see this, it means this

When you see thisWhat it may meanWhat to do next
Variation chart out of controlThe short-term variation estimate is unstable.Investigate variation signals first; delay conclusions from the mean chart.
One point beyond a control limitA large special cause may have occurred.Trace the exact time to material, equipment, setup, measurement, and operating records.
Nine points on one sideThe process level may have shifted.Identify a change point, verify whether it was intentional, and consider staged limits.
Six points steadily increasingA drift may be occurring.Check wear, thermal buildup, tool condition, calibration drift, or progressive material change.
P or U limits change each subgroupSample size or opportunity varies.Do not force constant limits; verify the subgroup-size column is correct.
Many P/U signals with huge samplesOverdispersion, underdispersion, or mixed process structure may exist.Investigate the structure, then assess Laney P′ or U′ if appropriate.
G/T point becomes very lowRare adverse events are occurring closer together.Escalate the investigation even though low values may look visually “better” on ordinary charts.
T² signal with stable univariate chartsThe joint combination of variables is unusual.Examine contributions, correlations, and process context rather than dismissing the signal.
All points inside limits but process misses specsThe process may be stable but incapable.Perform capability analysis after confirming stability.
