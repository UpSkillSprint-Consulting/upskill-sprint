# Steel Phase Explorer — Step 9 pilot and release gates

Step 9 is a controlled-release gate, not a declaration that testing has already happened. Browser entries are user-attested pointers to controlled source records; they are not independent proof.

## Student pilot exit criteria

- At least 90% route success.
- Median time to first meaningful interaction no more than 60 seconds.
- At least 85% lesson completion.
- Mean learning gain of at least 20 percentage points.
- Mean System Usability Scale score of at least 80.

## Professional pilot exit criteria

- At least 90% complete the comparison/report task successfully.
- Zero critical errors.
- Models remain versioned, out-of-domain results fail closed, and replay verification remains available.
- Independent plant validation is referenced.
- A qualified metallurgist is named with the approved scope.

## Product gates

- Preserve all 12 original modules and identical calculations across workspace modes.
- Preserve scenarios, saved state, links, exports, and numerical results except a separately versioned correction.
- Meet WCAG 2.2 AA, including keyboard operation and accessible chart summaries or tables.
- Confirm chart response around 150 ms against the agreed test device and dataset.
- Visually verify 360, 390, 768, 1024, and 1440 px layouts.
- Resolve all serious defects before controlled release.

The exported readiness JSON records the evaluated checklist and criteria version. Release owners must verify every referenced source record outside the browser before authorization.
