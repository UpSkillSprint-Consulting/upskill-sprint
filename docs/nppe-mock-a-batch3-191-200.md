## Source and content audit

Reviewed every stem, key, alternative and explanation for a single defensible answer under the explicit facts. All scenario facts and numerical examples are author-created. No instructor test bank or recalled exam questions were used.

- **191:** [Geoscientists Canada, The Profession](https://geoscientistscanada.ca/profession.php), applied Earth-science roles, fresh water and environmental protection. The item tests high-level scope, not a nationwide exclusive-practice rule or an individual's licensing entitlement.
- **192:** [Bentham's primary ethical text, reproduced by Fordham](https://sourcebooks.web.fordham.edu/mod/1789Bentham-utility.asp), chapter IV II–V, considers uncertainty and extent of consequences. This is historical philosophy, not Canadian legislation. The narrowly stated expected-benefit model yields **60 versus 45 household-hours**. Equal welfare significance, costs and other effects are explicit; safety and rights requirements are satisfied. Neither an outcome guarantee nor a general permission to override professional duties is implied.
- **193:** Current official indexed [Ontario Regulation 941](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/regulation/900941), s.77(7)(v), and [PEO's explanation of credit for altered work](https://www.peo.on.ca/sites/default/files/2019-07/Can%20engineering%20design%20plans%20be%20altered%20by%20another%20practitioner%3F.pdf). Direct e-Laws retrieval returned 403; indexed current statutory wording was checked. Reuse permission is stipulated, so copyright permission and professional authorship credit are not conflated. No finding of a particular disciplinary offence is asserted.
- **194:** [ISO House Style](https://www.iso.org/ISO-house-style.html), “Informative and normative labels,” read directly. An informative annex may contain requirements within an optional method, although that method need not be followed to claim conformity. The item deliberately states that mandatory clauses allow the alternative and no adopting law/contract separately requires the annex. It does not say informative annexes can never contain requirements.
- **195:** [PEO software-tools guideline](https://www.peo.on.ca/sites/default/files/2019-07/Professional%20Engineers%20Using%20Software-Based%20Engineering%20Tools.pdf), §§5.4–5.6, printed pp.6–7, read directly. The [current PEO index](https://www.peo.on.ca/knowledge-centre/practice-advice-resources-and-guidelines/practice-guidelines) still lists this 2011 guideline; its age is not hidden. Updated software can affect existing functions. Qualified documented corporate QA is permitted; the item does not require every engineer personally to repeat the entire test suite.
- **196:** [PEO Certificate of Authorization Requirements, March 2025](https://www.peo.on.ca/sites/default/files/2019-09/CofA_Infoguide.pdf), printed pp.4–5, distinguishes claim and aggregate limits/reinstatement. The policy and amounts here are expressly fictional, not minimum legal coverage. With no deductible, limit-eroding defence costs, reinstatement or excess insurance, the new payment is **min($900,000, $1,000,000, $2,000,000 − $1,400,000) = $600,000**. The unpaid liability is not extinguished by the limit.
- **197:** [Civil Code of Québec](https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/en/document/cs/ccq-1991), art.1470. Current official [French article text](https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/fr/version/lc/ccq-1991?code=se%3A1470) was verified through the primary site's search index; the official English article text was also checked through indexed results. Direct HTML/PDF retrieval failed (502/access errors), so no fresh full-consolidation reading is claimed. Both unforeseeability and irresistibility are necessary. Known seasonal closure and an affordable timely alternative defeat the asserted ground; the item does not declare every supply disruption ineligible or decide other remedies. No modifying force-majeure clause is assumed. The current article record shows its original 1994 commencement; that is not an asserted 2026 amendment.
- **198:** [CBCA s.45(1)](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/c-44/page-6.html), read directly, consolidation current to September 21, 2026. Passive shareholder status is separated from personal guarantees, wrongdoing, director obligations and statutory exceptions, all expressly excluded. No immunity for an engineer's own professional negligence is suggested.
- **199:** [EGBC individual compliance-audit guide v1.1, September 18, 2025](https://www.egbc.ca/getmedia/cf5ba026-bbf8-4cd6-8b8a-64faad38ade3/Guide-to-the-Compliance-Audit-Program-for-Individual-Registrants-V1-1.pdf), §§3.1.4.2–3.1.4.3, printed pp.5–6, read directly. Assessors are EGBC staff; the fictional relationship is a former partnership and active personal litigation. Program staff review supporting reasons and arrange mitigation/reassignment. The registrant cannot cancel the audit or appoint a substitute. The question concerns an individual compliance audit, not a disciplinary hearing or a technical practice-review finding.
- **200:** [Engineers Canada ethics guideline](https://engineerscanada.ca/guidelines-and-papers/public-guideline-on-the-code-of-ethics), principle 4 and its sensitivity-analysis interpretation. The national guideline is advisory. Council's decision authority and compliance of both options are stipulated; the task is transparent public-interest advice, not an invented Canada-wide rule about municipal powers. A technical ranking may be conditional on value choices.

All primary mappings were checked against the [official NPPE blueprint](https://www.nppexam.ca/en/preparation/blueprint/), which still labels 2017 current with a pending-review notice. I.3 includes geoscience definitions; I.2 includes public trust; VI.2 covers individual practice oversight. V is intentionally absent from this batch and prioritized next. Recheck the blueprint before release.

The supplied Andrews et al. (2019) and Samuels/Sanders (2007) references, documented in the Diagnostic and Learning audits, remain conceptual background. No new page-level textbook reading is claimed. Current primary authorities, rather than historical textbook rules, support the legal items.

## Originality and editorial qualifications

Screened all **410 existing** Diagnostic, Learning and Mock A/D/E stems and scenario families. Shared syllabus principles recur; the decision scenarios differ. Preliminary contractor-status and common-cause-failure ideas were rejected because those decisions are already sampled.

- 191 concerns the interpretive scope of geoscience, unlike 071 national/regional organizations or 121 rings.
- 192 evaluates uncertainty and magnitude with supplied comparable benefits, unlike 082's duration/intensity versus headcount or 075's low-probability catastrophic hazard.
- 193 separates permission from attribution for an adapted method, unlike 042 credit for a subordinate's work in a sealed deliverable or 097 assignment and moral-rights waiver.
- 194 interprets optional content within an adopted standard, unlike 043 whether a permit can make a standard binding or 124 which model-code edition applies.
- 195 addresses regression assurance after an internal update, unlike 005 an observed benchmark discrepancy or 168 a new use outside a benchmark's operating regime.
- 196 addresses aggregate depletion, unlike 025 claims-made timing, 125 deductibles or 174 financial risk transfer.
- 197 addresses Quebec superior force, unlike 087 client resiliation or 148 pre-contract disclosure.
- 198 concerns unrelated corporate lease debt owed by a passive shareholder, unlike D580 an engineer's personal accountability for their own sealed work.
- 199 concerns assessor assignment in a routine audit, unlike 010 discipline-panel prejudgment or 130 non-practising status and audit exemption.
- 200 concerns ranking sensitivity to public priorities, unlike 112 public statements about causation or 163 a client's choice among acceptable services.

Review retained the limited insurance assumptions, the informative-annex qualification and discretionary conflict handling to avoid overbroad rules. No existing bank was rewritten.
