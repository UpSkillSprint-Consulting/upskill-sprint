## Source audit and qualifications

All options and rationales were reviewed against the explicit scenario facts. Primary sources below were accessed on 2026-10-16. No publisher instructor-bank or recalled exam questions were used.

- **211:** [Engineers Canada sustainability guideline](https://engineerscanada.ca/guidelines-and-papers/national-guideline-on-sustainable-development-and-environmental-stewardship-for-professional-engineers), §5.7, Guideline 7 commentary, directly read. Advisory guidance describes practical translation of science into technology. The verified reduction and absence of offsetting harm are scenario facts; no new discovery, patent or universal superiority is implied.
- **212:** [Engineers Canada ethics guideline](https://engineerscanada.ca/guidelines-and-papers/public-guideline-on-the-code-of-ethics), principle 5 interpretation, directly read. Its confidentiality discussion concerns specific privileged or proprietary information. The question expressly excludes contractual/IP restrictions and former-client material. It tests the public-information boundary, not a general legal right to reuse anything remembered after employment.
- **213:** [PEO's reproduction of Regulation 941, section 72](https://www.peo.on.ca/about-peo/what-peo/acts-regulations-and-laws/professional-misconduct-section-72-regulation-o-reg), §72(2)(i), item 1, directly read; current [Ontario e-Laws entry](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/regulation/900941) also surfaced the dual-compensation provision. The regulator's full text was the readable source. The exact provision requires prior disclosure; the item does not invent an absolute ban or assert that disclosure alone makes every arrangement permissible. Contractual consent and other conflict obligations remain separate.
- **214:** [CCOHS risk-assessment guidance](https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/hsprograms/hazard/risk_assessment.html), semi-quantitative methods and Table 3, directly read. It describes numerical category scoring for prioritization. The absence of ratio calibration is an explicit premise; the conclusion about the unsupported loss ratio follows from that premise, not a purported CCOHS ban on quantitative risk analysis.
- **215:** [Canadian Centre for Cyber Security access-control guidance](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/implement-access-control-and-authorization), directly read. Minimum task permissions support the selected read-only account. Guidance is not presented as a statute binding every private firm. The scenario expressly provides a suitable named account.
- **216:** [CFPOA consolidated primary text](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/c-45.2/FullText.html), §§3(1), 3(3)(a), and repealed 3(4)–(5), directly read. Express permission under foreign law is stipulated; local custom alone is insufficient. The removed facilitation exception is not revived. No assertion that every payment connected with government is bribery, or that every unexplained request proves a completed criminal offence.
- **217:** [PIPEDA primary text](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/p-8.6/page-1.html), §2(1) business-contact definition and §4.01, directly read. The sole professional-communication purpose is essential. Other laws, contractual duties and unrelated personal information are not excluded by this answer.
- **218:** [ASME Fellows](https://www.asme.org/about-asme/honors-awards/fellows) and [Engineers Canada FAQ, question 8](https://engineerscanada.ca/frequently-asked-questions), directly read. ASME describes recognition of individual engineering achievement; provincial and territorial regulators handle Canadian licensing. No numerical fellowship eligibility trivia, accreditation claim or regulatory exemption is introduced.
- **219:** [Health Canada QMRA guidance](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/environmental-workplace-health/reports-publications/water-quality/guidance-qmra-drinking-water.html), §B.2.2 and Box B4, directly read. Non-detect findings need not represent zero. A modelling substitution is not an observed concentration. This is communication of a source-water test, not drinking-water safety certification or a statutory averaging calculation.
- **220:** Engineers Canada ethics guideline, principle 5 conflict interpretation, directly read. The original scenario applies the broad conflict principle to an unpaid allegiance and an expressly conditional offer. It does not declare all charitable donations improper or make a criminal finding. Disclosure supports safeguards; recusal may be needed and is not ruled out.

Mappings were checked against the [official NPPE blueprint](https://www.nppexam.ca/en/preparation/blueprint/), still presenting the 2017 blueprint as current with a review notice. I.4 covers engineering's value; V.4 covers professional/technical societies; the remaining tags follow the primary decision being assessed. Existing Andrews et al.
