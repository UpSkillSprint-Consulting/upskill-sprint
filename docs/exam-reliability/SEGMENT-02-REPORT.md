## Decisions that prevent contradictory later implementations

Selections and saves do not automatically become graded retrievals. Core exam/quiz modes grade once at finalization; adaptive practice grades at explicit check. Blanks belong in the examination/domain denominator but not answered mastery. Lifetime-first, first-since-reset, current-bank and historical counts are separate scopes. Unknown/compacted provenance is preserved rather than turned into a guessed first answer. First/repeat is classified before filtering dates.

Historical exam length, site practice target, question/option order and all relevant versions are pinned. MBB175-question sessions cannot be filtered out using today's100-question generic length. A rounded70% display for115/165 does not satisfy a70% target. Raw accuracy, attempted mastery, raw coverage, weighted coverage and readiness retain distinct formulas. Existing mastery coefficients and scheduling behavior are pinned rather than adjusted to make the dashboard look higher.

Timed sessions continue while hidden/offline and cannot pause; reload cannot grant extra time. Untrustworthy offline clock recovery is explicit. Visible active question time includes revisits and known zeros, excludes hidden/paused time, and discloses unknown durations. Terminal transitions, idempotent retries, writer ownership, stale revisions and reset epochs have fixed outcomes. An adaptive reset does not erase lifetime evidence or release New-only exclusions. Synced-as-of requires relevant receipts and complete catch-up, not just a connected indicator.

The contracts are fixed for review, not falsely marked human-approved. Production modules must adopt the single policy/envelope in their assigned segment; the existing defect register remains open until those repairs and acceptance tests pass.
