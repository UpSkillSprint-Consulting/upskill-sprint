## Source audit and qualifications

Every option and rationale was checked against the stated facts. Questions are original scenarios, not publisher instructor-bank or recalled exam material. Sources were checked on 2026-10-16.

- **201:** [PEO reduced fee policy](https://www.peo.on.ca/licence-holders/licence-holder-services/reduced-fee-policy), Fee remission conditions, directly read. Valid licence plus approved remission permits the associated title but not practice. The case excludes suspension, cancellation and additional conditions. It does not generalize Ontario remission rules across Canada or make a biography a service offering.
- **202:** [Engineers Canada code-of-ethics guideline](https://engineerscanada.ca/guidelines-and-papers/public-guideline-on-the-code-of-ethics), principle 5 and interpretation, directly read. This is advisory national guidance, not federal legislation. The duplicate is confirmed and no other debt or advance arrangement exists. The task is honest correction, not a ruling on restitution litigation.
- **203:** [PEO Conducting a Practice Review, July 2014](https://www.peo.on.ca/sites/default/files/2019-10/Conducting-a-Practice-Review_1.pdf), §5.3.4, printed p.6, directly read: scope, communication and budget monitoring. **PEO's current guidelines index warns that listed material may be outdated and is undergoing review.** This older document is background practice guidance, not current legal authority. The binding spending cap and prior-approval term are expressly supplied in the scenario; no automatic right to additional fees or unpaid completion is asserted. Safe pausing excludes emergency-duty ambiguity.
- **204:** [Engineers Canada sustainability guideline](https://engineerscanada.ca/guidelines-and-papers/national-guideline-on-sustainable-development-and-environmental-stewardship-for-professional-engineers), §5.6, directly read. National advisory guidance supports lifecycle assessment. The fictional figures compare equal service over 20 years; other impacts are stipulated equal. A's 32 tonnes versus B's 44 tonnes is an emissions comparison, not proof that A is universally superior in every social or economic respect.
- **205:** [GPLv3 primary licence text reproduced by the Open Source Initiative](https://opensource.org/license/gpl-3.0), §§4–6, directly read. GNU-hosted text retrieval failed; the OSI-approved licence reproduction was read instead. GPLv3 permits charging and imposes corresponding-source conditions on object-code conveyance. The entire program is expressly covered: no uncertain linking/derivative-work boundary, alternative licence, exception or merely internal use. No claim that every company file must be publicly posted; the required source provision depends on the permitted delivery method.
- **206:** [Sable Offshore Energy Inc. v. Ameron International Corp., 2013 SCC 37](https://decisions.scc-csc.ca/scc-csc/scc-csc/en/item/13138/index.do), paras.12–14 and 19–20. The official SCC index exposed the relevant holding and exception discussion; direct HTML/PDF retrieval returned errors. No full judgment retrieval is claimed. Genuine confidential settlement negotiations are stipulated; absence of a label alone does not defeat privilege. Waiver, recognized exceptions and proof-of-settlement issues are excluded. This does not protect ordinary business correspondence simply because someone adds a heading.
- **207:** [Surety Association of Canada, Need a Contract Bond](https://suretycanada.com/SAC/SAC/Surety-Bonds/Need-a-Contract-Bond.aspx), roles and indemnity discussion, directly read. This is first-party industry guidance, not legislation. The valid, applicable indemnity is supplied as a fact. The payment is covered and no defence is in dispute. The question does not impose automatic personal liability on shareholders, spouses or employees.
- **208:** [Ontario OHSA](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/90o01), §§43(11)–(12), and the government's [Part V guide](https://www.ontario.ca/document/guide-occupational-health-and-safety-act/part-v-right-refuse-or-stop-work-where-health-and-safety-danger). Current official indexed Act text (Ontario's jobs.ontario.ca mirror) and guide text confirm disclosure of refusal/reasons in the appropriate representative's presence. Direct pages returned 403/errors; archived versioned results were not treated as current law. The pending-inspector stage and unrestricted industrial-workplace application are explicit. Disclosure is necessary, not proof of safety or removal of the second worker's applicable refusal right.
- **209:** [PEO Discipline Committee Rules, November 7, 2025](https://www.peo.on.ca/sites/default/files/2025-11/rules-of-procedure-dic-nov2025.pdf), rule 9.2, printed p.9, directly read. The merits hearing is distinct from a pre-hearing conference; statutory exceptions and tribunal/court orders are preserved. The question concerns the effect of a private contract, not whether a properly supported closure request would ultimately succeed.
- **210:** [Federal Courts Rules, Code of Conduct for Expert Witnesses](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/sor-98-106/page-118.html), schedule to rule 52.2, paras.1–2 and 5, directly read; consolidation current to 2026-09-21, last amended 2025-12-21. The expert must exercise independent judgment and clarify areas of agreement/disagreement. No requirement to split the difference, agree, or obey partisan tactical instructions is invented. This is a court-ordered expert conference, not settlement mediation.

Mappings were checked against the [official NPPE blueprint](https://www.nppexam.ca/en/preparation/blueprint/), which still identifies the 2017 blueprint as current while announcing a review. V.1 includes title rights; III.7 includes software copyright; IV.9 includes indemnities and bond roles; IV.8 includes the expert's role and neutrality. Recheck all current requirements before release.

Existing Andrews et al. (2019) and Samuels/Sanders (2007) textbook references remain conceptual background. No new page-level textbook inspection is claimed; current legal propositions use the sources and retrieval qualifications above.
