## Source review and boundaries

Sources checked on 2026-10-16. Guidance is identified by jurisdiction and authority; national advisory documents are not represented as binding rules across Canada. All four alternatives were reviewed for consistency with the stipulated facts. No publisher instructor-bank or recalled examination questions were used.

- **231:** [Engineers Canada good-character guideline](https://engineerscanada.ca/guidelines-and-papers/public-guidelines-on-good-character), December 2024, sections 2 and 3.2, directly read. Personal conduct can be relevant to continuing trustworthiness. The item establishes deliberate falsification for its hypothetical but does not infer a particular regulator's jurisdictional conclusion, finding or penalty. Repayment does not automatically erase the conduct.
- **232:** [Engineers Canada ethics guideline](https://engineerscanada.ca/guidelines-and-papers/public-guideline-on-the-code-of-ethics), principles 2 and 7, directly read. Collegiality and honest conviction coexist. The disagreement is reasonable and unresolved; there is no established misconduct or imminent danger. It does not authorize obstruction of a justified decision or substitute dissent for mandatory reporting.
- **233:** [PEO Professional Engineering Practice Guideline](https://www.peo.on.ca/sites/default/files/2019-11/ProfessionalEngineeringPracticeGuideline_0.pdf), displayed November 2020 edition, section 10.2, directly read. The guide supports integrity and explicit fee/scope/payment arrangements. The fixed-fee entitlement is a premise of this question, not a claim that the guide mandates a particular pricing model. Scope, checks and deliverables are complete; no hourly basis, savings-sharing clause, inaccurate estimate or extra-charge entitlement is assumed. A concession could be negotiated, but it is not mandatory on these facts.
- **234:** [EGBC Mechanical Engineering Services for Building Projects, Version 2.0](https://tools.egbc.ca/Practice-Resources/Individual-Practice/Guidelines-Advisories/Document/01525AMWY6RFNIFUCGTVDL2Z773FZMRAQG/Mechanical%20Engineering%20Services%20for%20Building%20Projects), sections 2.1.2 and 3.3.1, directly read. Clear responsibility, shared design inputs and discipline coordination support closing the identified gap. No single professional is asked to assume competence in every discipline. The scenario does not prescribe a fan-support design.
- **235:** National ethics guideline above, principle 7 interpretation, directly read. The other engineer is reachable and notification is authorized; litigation, restricted disclosures and urgent protective action are excluded. Notification does not confer control over the review. This is the national guideline's normal approach, not an assertion that every jurisdiction has identical statutory wording.
- **236:** [EGBC authentication guide, Version 5.0](https://www.egbc.ca/getmedia/bb8be418-ae51-4ea5-ad0d-2c671bcb1df0/EGBC-QMG-Authentication-of-Documents.pdf), **September 1, 2026**, sections 3.4.4.1 and 3.6.3.1–3.6.3.2, directly read. An older Version 3.0 surfaced in search and was not used. The original professional remains responsible, the revision is technical and the digital validation outcome is stipulated. The question does not claim that every possible PDF annotation invalidates every signature. Technical approval does not replace authentication of the issued revision, and supervision does not permit credential sharing.
- **237:** [Justice Canada section 35 guidance](https://justice.canada.ca/eng/csj-sjc/ijr-dja/35pedia-wiki35/p8.html), Crown obligation and fulfilling the duty sections, directly read; [Haida Nation, 2004 SCC 73](https://decisions.scc-csc.ca/scc-csc/scc-csc/en/item/2189/index.do), paragraphs 41–46, checked in indexed official judgment text. Direct court HTML/PDF retrieval failed; no full-judgment read is claimed. The federal executive decision and triggered, unfulfilled duty are stipulated. The question addresses a predetermined process, not the adequacy of a real consultation, project approval, a universal veto rule or the absence of additional consent requirements. Applicable statutory, treaty and other requirements must still be considered. The engineer cannot personally promise every accommodation on behalf of the Crown.
- **238:** [Guarantee Co. of North America v. Gordon Capital Corp., [1999] 3 S.C.R. 423](https://decisions.scc-csc.ca/scc-csc/scc-csc/en/item/1738/index.do), paragraphs 39–41, especially 40, checked in indexed official judgment text; the indexed court PDF independently contains the same matured-rights passage. Direct page retrieval failed. The scenario stipulates valid acceptance, an already-payable fee and no set-off, waiver, settlement, rescission or applicable exception. It tests the distinction between ending future performance and erasing prior rights, not how to elect a remedy or calculate damages in a real dispute.
- **239:** [EGBC firm audit guide, Version 1.0](https://www.egbc.ca/getmedia/e2dba053-73e1-4660-9097-4590b13f7be2/Guide-to-the-Compliance-Audit-Program-for-Registrant-Firms.pdf), introduction and section 3.1, directly read. It expressly recognizes superseding legal and ethical reporting obligations. The reporting trigger is stipulated; no blanket publication right or final disciplinary outcome is inferred from referral.
- **240:** [Engineers Canada Washington Accord page](https://engineerscanada.ca/accreditation/the-washington-accord) and [licensing overview](https://engineerscanada.ca/become-an-engineer/overview-of-licensing-process), directly read.
