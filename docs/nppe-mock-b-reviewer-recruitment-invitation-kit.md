## Scope note supplied before discussing confidential materials

UpSkillSprint is developing **original, unofficial NPPE preparation items**. We are seeking qualified independent specialists to evaluate a 110-question Mock B form (100 intended scored items, 10 simulated unscored; candidate-facing questions have four choices). This is **not** a request to disclose actual NPPE questions, confidential NPPE examination content, previously seen official questions, publisher instructor items or protected regulator materials.

No questions or answer keys should be shared before agreeing on independence, confidentiality, authorized access and a project-owner-approved engagement. The review will require item-specific written assessments, source verification and separately documented adjudication. Approval is never contingent on agreeing with the original author.

## Private qualification-screening questions

1. What professional registration(s), licensing status and relevant jurisdiction(s) can we independently verify, if the role is regulated?
2. Which areas of engineering practice, Canadian law, assessment development, accessible assessment or psychometrics fall within your current competence?
3. Have you authored, previously reviewed, used or had privileged access to any of these original UpSkillSprint Mock B items, or do you have a financial or other conflict?
4. Can you undertake blind first-pass selection before viewing answers where applicable, with a separate written review of all four options?
5. For legal review, can you document exact controlling statute/regulation versions, case paragraphs, effective date and jurisdiction?
6. Can you securely handle restricted question/answer content under the applicable agreement and record reviewed versions, evidence, dated findings and signed decisions?
7. What is your availability and preferred engagement arrangement? Do not promise a fixed schedule, fee or item quota before the scope and agreement are approved.
8. If you cannot review a topic within your licence/specialty, can you promptly identify the limitation and request another reviewer?

**Do not store responses to this questionnaire in GitHub.** The private review roster has vacant specialty pools; fill the roster only after authentic identity/credential verification and consent to the scope. Do not solicit lawyers from restricted professional directories contrary to their terms.
