## Primary-source review and qualifications

Sources were checked on 2026-10-16. Each option and rationale was reviewed against the stated facts. Questions are original, not publisher instructor-bank or recalled exam material.

- **221:** [EGBC Use of Title in Registrant Firms bulletin](https://www.egbc.ca/getmedia/eb2971ce-4d65-4ea5-aff4-29bbb23f4bd3/2025-01-23-ROF-and-Restricted-Titles-Final.pdf), document dated **November 14, 2025**, pp.1–2, directly read. The filename date is not the bulletin's displayed issue date. It reproduces Bylaw 5.12(22)(a) and explains 7.7.3(1)(f), including the PPMP and title-assignment responsibilities. The regulator's [November 27, 2025 enforcement notice](https://www.egbc.ca/News/Articles/Enforcement-of-Title-for-Firms-Starts-January-1) confirms firm enforcement from January 1, 2026. The item uses the expressly reserved P.Eng. designation, an unregistered employee and no exception; it does not generalize every use of the word engineer. This is the firm's responsibility, not a claim that supervised employees may never perform engineering work.
- **222:** [Engineers Canada ethics guideline](https://engineerscanada.ca/guidelines-and-papers/public-guideline-on-the-code-of-ethics), principle 13 interpretation, directly read: accurate experience representations and honest advertising. National guidance is advisory. Public use of the project name is permitted; the false personal leadership claim is the issue, not confidentiality or copyright ownership.
- **223:** Same national guideline, principles 4, 7 and 10, directly read. The scenario separates an unresolved lead from a supported finding. Prompt clarification does not mean waiting for conclusive proof or postponing an already-triggered statutory duty. No imminent hazard is identified; the jurisdiction metadata expressly excludes deferral of an existing reporting deadline. The selected option retains required reporting and accuracy. BC statutory duty-to-report provisions were reviewed as a boundary check but are not substituted for the item's national ethical framing.
- **224:** [EGBC Guide to the Standard for Retention of Project Documentation](https://www.egbc.ca/getmedia/114e91a9-402e-4ab6-af2e-b7b84b3a2fcb/EGBC-QMG-Retention-of-Docs.pdf), indexed September 1, 2026 edition, §3.2.5. Current regulator-hosted indexed text confirms that litigation and other legal issues may require postponing destruction; direct PDF retrieval returned an error. No full-document read is claimed. The applicable hold is expressly confirmed by counsel in the scenario, rather than inferred from age or a universal limitation period. The key does not require indefinite retention of every record.
- **225:** [CCOHS Hierarchy of Controls](https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/hsprograms/hazard/hierarchy_controls.html), substitution and control-review sections, directly read. Assess alternative hazards and prevent an equally or more harmful substitution. The toxicity and fire findings are premises. The item does not prescribe an engineering fire-protection design or assert that substitution is always unacceptable.
- **226:** [Fisheries Act](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/F-14/section-36.html), §§36(3)–(4), directly read; consolidation current to September 30, 2026. The substance, receiving water and absence of applicable authorization are stipulated. The rule is not described as an absolute ban on all discharges; the statutory regulatory pathways are acknowledged. No fish kill must first be observed on these facts.
- **227:** [Competition Act](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/c-34/section-47.html), §§47(1)(b), (2) and (3), directly read; consolidation current to September 30, 2026. A genuine tender call, unaffiliated firms, submitted coordinated bids, nondisclosure and no applicable exception are explicit. This is not a prohibition on every disclosed consortium or ordinary subcontract. The item identifies the legal character of stipulated conduct, not a real person's guilt or sentencing outcome.
- **228:** [Patent Act](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/P-4/section-32.html), §32, directly read after retrying the canonical capitalized Act path; consolidation current to September 21, 2026. The original and improvement patents are valid/in force, the base invention is necessarily used, and no licence or exception applies. Patentability and permission are distinct. [CIPO external IP audit guidance](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canadian-intellectual-property-office/en/build-intellectual-property-strategy-do-external-ip-audit) independently supports separating ownership from freedom-to-operate analysis.
- **229:** [APEGS On-Demand Ethics Modules](https://www.apegs.ca/cpd/pd-opportunities/online-ethics-modules), opening guidance and current-year verification notes, directly read. The modules are optional routes; other activities must still qualify. This does not waive annual requirements, reporting, verification or any individualized course order. No cross-regulator transfer of CPD credit is assumed.
- **230:** [DFO Policy for applying measures to offset harmful impacts to fish and fish habitat](https://www.dfo-mpo.gc.ca/pnw-ppe/reviews-revues/policies-politiques-eng.html), current page updated May 25, 2026, Part 1 principles 1, 3 and 8 and Part 2 habitat construction, directly read. Avoidance/mitigation precede offsets; outcomes and time lags matter. Policy is guidance, not a substitute for legislation or an approval. Equal area alone cannot substantiate the claim on the supplied functional and timing facts. The item does not conclude that habitat construction is categorically forbidden or that this project is authorizable. Older conservation-allowance material and a draft biodiversity policy were screened but not used as current governing authority.

The [official NPPE blueprint](https://www.nppexam.ca/en/preparation/blueprint/) still presents the 2017 blueprint with a review notice. The procurement item was remapped during drafting from IV.14 to **IV.2**, whose listed topics include the formal tendering and bid process. V.3 concerns title misuse; III.9 concerns truthful reporting; III.8 includes record keeping; III.6 concerns professional environmental judgment. All four V topics are now sampled in Mock A. This does not establish exhaustive coverage.

Supplied Andrews et al. (2019) and Samuels/Sanders (2007) references remain conceptual background. No new page-level textbook inspection or independent SME sign-off is claimed. Recheck sources before release.
