## Executive conclusion

**Editorial content review: completed with corrective changes.** All 50 original, four-option single-answer questions have been inspected for their intended answer, plausible alternative responses, blueprint label, professional/Canadian legal principle, wording, provisional difficulty and source provenance. Multiple improvements were committed; the reviewed package is **not yet psychometrically validated or independently professionally certified**. It must not be called an official or guaranteed NPPE pass predictor.

**Recommendation:** Retain the draft and `coming_soon` publication status. The 50-item Diagnostic may progress to independent legal/engineering-geoscience SME review and controlled piloting, but is **not yet approved for full public release**.

### Substantive corrections and defects found

1. **Systemic answer-length cue (high):** In the original authored bank, 36 of 50 correct alternatives were the longest. Reworked 26 keyed-choice phrasings and several wrong-option phrasings; the correct option is now longest in 12 of 50, close to an uninformative proportion. Key identities did not change.
2. **Unrealistic distractors (moderate):** Rewrote incorrect choices and corresponding rationale text for 006, 010, 012, 013, 016, 020, 028, 034 and 044 to require more considered judgments without creating competing correct answers.
3. **Near-duplicate learning objective (moderate):** Replaced 015's second 'unfamiliar specialty assignment' scenario (overlapping 004) with a distinct professional peer-review, consultation and collaboration case under III.3. The intended key B was retained; all options, explanations and references were updated.
4. **Overstated provisional difficulty (moderate):** Downgraded 012, 013, 016, 020, 028 and 044 from hard to medium. Remaining difficulty labels are editorial hypotheses, not NPPE calibration.
5. **Provenance ambiguity (moderate):** Added explicit source-type categories to older Batch 1–2 items, and relabelled Engineers Canada national ethics/sustainability publications as non-binding guidance rather than 'primary law'. Insurance-program FAQs are not presented as contract terms or legislation.
6. **Earlier subtopic discrepancies (resolved before this audit):** Seven Batch 2 metadata corrections for 013, 014, 015, 016, 017, 018 and 020 remain guarded by regression tests. Stem/answer domains are unchanged.
7. **Legal scope (ongoing control):** Ontario expert duty, Quebec civil law, Saskatchewan environmental/OHS/title rules and BC auditing/CE requirements are attributed to their own jurisdictions. Current legal sources take precedence over the 2007/2019 textbooks.
8. **External source verification limitation:** Ontario's canonical Rules of Civil Procedure URL returned a browser 403 during direct retrieval; the Rule 4.1.01 text was also found through indexed Ontario regulatory versions and corroborated by legal secondary sources. Mark for independent current-statute confirmation before public release.

## Source and current-law verification

**Source hierarchy:** the NPPE's officially published **2017 blueprint** establishes scope (the website still labels it current with an anticipated revision notice); the user-supplied Andrews et al. (2019) and Samuels/Sanders (2007) establish concepts; contemporary legislation, government sources and regulator pages establish current jurisdictional obligations; Engineers Canada's national guidance and technical-society materials are clearly **non-regulatory guidance** unless adopted by a regulator.

**Representative binding/current-source crosschecks:**

- [NPPE current blueprint](https://www.nppexam.ca/en/preparation/blueprint/) — 40 numbered subtopics and six domain ranges; the 50-item bank covers 36 as primary labels.
- [APEGS Practice and Title](https://www.apegs.ca/complaints-enforcement/practice-and-title) and [Code of Ethics](https://www.apegs.ca/complaints-enforcement/code-of-ethics) — items 033 and 039.

## Remaining gaps — do not mislabel as complete validation

- **IV.1, IV.10, IV.13, IV.14** are not represented by a dedicated primary question; ensure separate Learning and Mock A–C coverage before claiming blueprint-wide mastery.
- **Difficulty and discriminatory power:** no first-exposure candidate response statistics, item-total discrimination, distractor functioning, reliability or equating study exists. The Diagnostic score **cannot guarantee real NPPE success**.
- **Independent legal/engineering/geoscience review:** required especially for jurisdiction-sensitive items 025, 028, 030, 033, 037, 039, 040, 046–050. Automated and model-based review cannot substitute for professional assessment.
- **Deployed learner/admin access test:** GitHub browser workflows use explicit isolated permission/account fixtures, not real user approval/revocation. Previously acknowledged source/history exposure is not fixed by private delivery.
- **Controlled release:** PR #301 remains draft, NPPE remains `coming_soon`, and no Supabase migration or production access grant is authorized.

## Test evidence

The test suite checks question count/IDs, complete all-option rationales, exact metadata, answer distribution/length-cue regression, batch ordering, idempotent registration, bank ownership, 69-minute and untimed sessions, raw scoring and review only after submission, question-reveal prevention and source-isolated protected delivery. The latest CI commit must be checked explicitly before sign-off; a passing historical run is not final-revision evidence.

**Decision:** Editorial content audit complete; retain **DRAFT / NOT RELEASE-READY** pending independent SME/legal review, empirical pilot and live-account access verification.
