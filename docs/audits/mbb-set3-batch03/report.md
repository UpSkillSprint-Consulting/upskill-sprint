## Numerical validation

**Q62 — capacity.** The explicit 10 Belt-hours per project per week converts allocations 3/2/1/3 to loads 30/20/10/30. Four 20-hour capacities total 80 against 90 demanded. Two local excesses total 20 hours, while another Belt has 10 spare hours; the net deficit is 10. Reassignment cannot erase aggregate excess. This is a simplified planning case, not a universal concurrent-project limit.

**Q63 — common cash-flow horizon.** M: 300,000/150,000 = 2.0 benefit–cost ratio and $150,000 first-year net value. N: 900,000/600,000 = 1.5 and $300,000. N exceeds M by $150,000 in that stated year, not a proven recurring annual difference. Later cash flows are unspecified.

**Q64 — network and float.** Parallel activities finish at days 3, 4 and 6. The 1-day readiness review starts at day 6 and finishes at day 7. Supplier total float is 6−4 = 2 days; material testing float is 3 days. The final milestone takes zero days. Finish-to-start links, no lags, adequate resources and unchanged durations are explicit.

**Q70 — profitability index and capital rationing.** Standard PI values are 1.90, 1.88 and 1.80. The previous 0.90/0.88/0.80 values were NPV/investment, not the defined PI. All eight subsets were enumerated: six meet the $500,000 limit. Projects 1+3 cost $500,000 and yield $420,000 NPV; Projects 1+2 cost $450,000 and yield $400,000; Projects 2+3 cost $550,000 and are infeasible. Greedy PI ranking is not an optimizer for this indivisible case. The full enumeration is in `independent-calculations.json`.

No inferential p-values, degrees of freedom, confidence intervals, DOE aliases, capability indices or reliability calculations occur in these 25 items. Those checks are not applicable, not silently counted as passes.
