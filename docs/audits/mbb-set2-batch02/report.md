### Numerical and technical findings

Q36’s screening model now explicitly distinguishes realized net NPV from zero net NPV in the other outcome, includes prerequisites in resource demand, and prevents double-counting prerequisite probabilities. Exhaustive enumeration gives the unique 12-FTE optimum A+B+C at 2.95 million expected NPV; the closest feasible alternative A+C+D is 2.83 million. This is a stipulated educational model, not a general rule for valuing real projects.

Q37 independently recomputes to SPI=0.80, CPI=0.8888889 (0.89), schedule variance −120,000 and cost variance −60,000. The key was already correct.

Q40’s grid has ten required role-skill targets. The corrected explanation names each accurately. Competency levels are ordered categories, not equal numerical intervals; unrequired cells do not imply a training gap.

Q46’s original summary did not define its agreement denominator. The revision supplies a coherent synthetic two-trial study, not purported recovered raw data. For A/B/C, the within-inspector rates are 96/94/98%, and reference-correct individual-rating rates are 82/83/97%. The pooled reference rate is 262/300 = 87.3%. Counts are retained for reproducible checks. The 90% criterion is explicitly inspector-specific and based on point estimates, not confidence bounds. The table no longer prints the pass/fail decision for the candidate.

Q48’s response at (−1,1) is 72 and its gradient is (18,−6); local ascent is therefore in direction 3:−1 in comparable coded units. Exact contour intercepts and finite-difference derivatives agree. The chart now uses identical pixel distances per coded unit on both axes and separates the current-setting label from the contours.

Q49’s unrounded probabilities are component 0.7764816931, series 0.6029238198 and active-parallel 0.9500395665. Rounding once to three places produces .776/.603/.950. The item now states hours, independent mission failures, nonrepairability and the full-load capability needed for the active-parallel formula.

Q50 already distinguished a binding resource from a known shadow price. An independent counterexample verifies that a binding redundant constraint can have zero marginal value.
