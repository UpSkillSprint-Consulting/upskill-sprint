### Quantitative verification

**Q105:** R requires 3 Belt-months, S requires 5 and discovery requires 1: total **9**. Funding all four proposals would require 13 and still would not satisfy U's unbuilt prerequisite. Indicative downstream value is not treated as currently realizable value. The slider has a labelled fixed scored case and reset.

**Q106:** Customer demand and upstream orders both total 1,000 units over ten weeks, with mean 100. Sample standard deviations are **1.414214** and **41.376322 units**, respectively. The new dataset illustrates amplification; it is not an estimate from a real supply chain or proof that the rule was the only cause.

**Q110:** The original annualized submitted and validated estimates are **$5.2 million and $2.7 million** against a $4.0 million target. The validation shortfall is **$1.3 million**, not established realized cash loss. Training counts remain 96 versus 80; control adherence 54% versus 85%; owner acceptance 61% versus 90%.

**Q111:** A–B–D–E gives **2 + 4 + 3 + 3 = 12 working weeks**. A–C–E is 10 weeks; C has **2 weeks of float** in the stipulated network. E waits for both prerequisite branches.

**Q114:** Expected annual net inflow is **0.70 × $210,000 + 0.30 × $60,000 = $165,000**. Expected NPV is **−$300,000 + $165,000/1.10 + $165,000/1.10² = −$13,636.3636**, or **−$13,600** to the specified nearest $100. Independently probability-weighting the two discounted scenario NPVs gives the same result. Distractors correspond to omitting discounting (+$30,000), using only the high scenario (approximately +$64,500), or omitting Year 2 (−$150,000).

**Q115:** The infrastructure gaps are **18 and 11.5 percentage points**, while the observed technique gap is **1 percentage point**. They are different constructs, not additive causal contributions.

**Q116/Q119:** There are **16 randomized teams and 320 observed workers** in Q116. Q119 consumes **60 distinct coupons**. Its residual cannot by itself identify separate measurement and coupon variance components. The calculation record includes an explicitly arbitrary numerical identifiability counterexample, not claimed study estimates.

**Q120/Q122/Q123:** The Q120 histogram contains **128 observations**, with **20 above 18 hours (15.625% empirically)** under the stated (lower, upper] convention. No lognormal parameters are supplied, so no numerical model tail is fabricated. For Q122, **1.96/√68 = 0.2376849**, displayed as 0.24; supplied lag-1 and lag-2 values exceed the bounds. The absent residual series prevents an independent reconstruction of the ACF itself. Q123's two displayed curves meet at **1,200 hours and 0.68 reliability**; event/censor records are absent, so no formal proportional-hazards p-value is asserted.

**Q124:** The 24-row model matrix has rank **12**. Error degrees of freedom are **4 whole-plot** and **12 subplot**; the complete decomposition is **23 = 1 temperature + 4 whole-plot error + 3 speed + 3 interaction + 12 subplot error**.

**Q125:** With x = (backlog − 100)/10, the fitted new-minus-standard contrast is **−2 + 0.6x**. At backlogs 80 and 140 it is **−3.2 and +0.4 minutes**. These are fitted conditional contrasts, not significance, equivalence or causal claims.
