## Independent quantitative and technical verification

**Q155 — Portfolio feasibility.** All 16 subsets were enumerated. B+C totals **9 Belt-months, $220,000 funding and $410,000 risk-adjusted value**, correcting three erroneous numbers in its distractor and the repeated erroneous value in the explanation/formula. AC is 10/$270k/$500k; AB is 9/$250k/$510k. **BD is the unique optimum at 10/$280k/$540k**, honoring D's dependency. Benefits are explicitly additive on a common basis, with no unlisted sequencing constraint. Feasible single projects are not falsely described as infeasible. The slider no longer names BD before answering, and a reset preserves the scored capacity of 10.

**Q161 — Earned value.** CPI = 480/550 = **0.8727272727**; SPI = 480/600 = **0.8**. Using unrounded CPI, EAC = **$1.375 million**, reported as **$1.38 million** to two decimals. Cost variance = **−$70,000**, schedule variance = **−$120,000** (earned-value currency, not elapsed time). Updating a forecast does not itself authorize rebaselining.

**Q165 — Attribution.** Perfectly coincident training, software and incentive indicators are identical columns: their three-column matrix has rank 1, so their separate coefficients are not identified. Adding those indicators to a regression cannot isolate the historical training contribution. Future design must vary training separately from otherwise comparable interventions, subject to safe implementation; descriptive change is not retrospectively converted into a causal estimate.

**Q168 — Destructive measurement study.** The specified allocation is **3 appraisers × 5 batches each × 2 distinct coupons per batch = 30 destroyed coupons**. Residual plus appraiser fractions sum to **24% of the reported variance**; the corresponding conditional study-variation proportion is **sqrt(0.24) = 48.9898%**, not 24%. Without defensible within-batch homogeneity, the residual includes specimen variability, so the sum is not established as pure measurement variation. No missing raw readings were invented to refit the supplied components. Destruction alone does not force a nested rather than crossed allocation.

**Q170 — Regression evidence.** The original chart omitted the confidence interval mentioned in the stem and displayed the proposed x=105 response as though it were an observation. The correction preserves the **eight original observed pairs**, removes the unsupported proposed response of 78, and independently fits ordinary least squares:

- Intercept = **17.001635323**; slope = **0.632215863**.
- Residual df = **6**, SSE = **5.926737531**, MSE = **0.9877895884**.
- Prediction leverage at x=105 = **1.5605069501**; new-point leverage need not be bounded by one like an in-sample hat-matrix diagonal.
- Predicted mean = **83.384300899 ($000)**.
- Conditional **95% mean-response CI: 80.346331464–86.422270335 ($000)**.
- Conditional **95% individual prediction interval: 79.492832340–87.275769459 ($000)**.

The normal-error, constant-variance linear-model assumptions are explicit. The mean interval is rendered and distinguished from an individual prediction interval. The unobserved proposal uses a distinct marker and label, not an observed-case dot. Neither conditional interval validates linearity outside the observed 10–70 range. Two equivalent tables expose the observed/proposed distinction and model output.

**Q171 — Logistic interaction.** For binary treatment T and centered severity s, the conditional odds ratio is **exp(beta_T + beta_TS × s)**, not one universal OR. Outcome coding and the reference value are defined. The significance/practical-importance statement is stipulated evidence; no coefficients or raw data exist to reconstruct a p-value, and none is invented. Odds are not probabilities and the observational association is not automatically causal.

**Q172 — Series reliability.** Independent nonrepairable subsystems give **0.98 × 0.95 × 0.97 = 0.90307** at the fixed mission. Equal absolute improvements of 0.01 yield gains **0.009215**, **0.009506**, **0.009310** respectively. Improving subsystem B gives the largest gain, reaching **0.912576**. The unsupported intermediate lifetime curves were removed; the visual now contains only the three supplied end-of-mission reliabilities and their equivalent table.

**Q174 — Aliasing and augmentation.** From I=ABCD, AB and CD have the same design column. Reversing **D only** gives the complementary fraction and separates their contrasts; reversing **all four** signs preserves ABCD and repeats the same fraction. The intercept/main-effects/two-factor model has rank **8** in the original fraction and **11** after the single-D foldover. The four supplied cell means imply an aliased contrast of **−15 response units**, not an independently identified AB effect or a significance test. Eight coded design rows are derived from the defining relation; no raw responses or p-values are invented. The plotted axis now actually spans its declared **55–85** domain, with distinct disclosure labels for the means table and the design table.

`independent-calculations.json` retains all subsets, data pairs, design rows, calculations and limits. These are independent computations from supplied evidence, not assertions that source authors published the same illustrative numbers.
