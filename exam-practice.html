Readiness teaser (from free public lessons)

  1. On a control chart, control limits come from the process (typically ±3σ), while specification limits come from the customer. True or false?
    Show answer

    True. Control limits describe the process; specification limits come from the customer.

    (Control Charts, Explained)
  2. If Cp = Cpk the process is perfectly centred. What does Cp > Cpk tell you?
    Show answer

    Cp > Cpk means the process is off-centre.

    (Process Capability)
  3. In a hypothesis test you start with the null of no difference and only reject it when the evidence is strong enough. What does a small p-value mean?
    Show answer

    A small p-value means data this extreme would be unlikely if the null were true.

    (Hypothesis Testing for Beginners)
