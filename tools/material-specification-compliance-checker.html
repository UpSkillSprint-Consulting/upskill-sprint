Material Specification Compliance Checker

Check certificate results against the attached ASTM A36, CSA G40.21 and CSA Z245.1 editions, or enter additional controlled requirements. Every result shows its acceptance rule and evidence basis.

How to use
Current decisionNot assessed0% evidence coverage
!

Important engineering-use notice

The attached editions provide audited, conditional screening requirements. Confirm the exact source edition, product and order context, referenced requirements, and documentary evidence before release. The result supports engineering review; certification and contract acceptance remain the responsibility of the authorized reviewer.

1

Define the comparison

Material and specification scope

Identify the material, source certification, controlled target requirement, product form, and applicable dimensions.

2

Select evidence

Independent compliance workbench

Choose sections for actual inputs and additional controlled requirements. Applicable attached-standard checks always run, including when these sections are deselected.

3

Enter evidence and limits

Actual results and target requirements

Use Load standard input rows for audited screening, or add acceptance limits from an additional controlled source. Existing actuals are retained when standard requirements are refreshed.

Row-level units take priority. Each actual result and requirement has its own unit selection.

4

Review the evidence

Compliance decision

Conservative gating is used: invalid inputs block a verdict, controlled failures cause Fail, and unresolved evidence causes Conditional.
