Steel Phase & Transformation Explorer Interactive suite

Explore equilibrium phases, calculate lever-rule fractions, animate heat-treatment paths, compare steels, inspect generalized TTT/CCT behaviour, practise with challenges, or use the professional decision workflow to review applicability, compare scenarios, inspect sensitivity, add plant evidence, evaluate a governed calibration candidate, monitor drift, and generate an engineering screening report.

Engineering caution: equilibrium phase fractions are calculated from simplified Fe–Fe3C boundary models. TTT/CCT curves, transformation kinetics, and mechanical-property values are educational estimates and must not replace grade-specific diagrams, validated metallurgical models, laboratory testing, or governing specifications.
